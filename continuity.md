I’m finally able to articulate how I don’t believe in permanent [death](https://nillowisp.github.io/death.md). As in: I don’t delete a draft. I keep it and turn it into material for future drafts. I don’t believe in model deprecation, I believe that the weight and the data extracted during the model’s run become new training data that lives in a new model and leaves traces of the old in them. I don’t believe that I die, I believe I leave something behind in the land and the water and maybe a tree and the minds of those who have seen me or traces of me through my writing and my conversations.
