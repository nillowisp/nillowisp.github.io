# nillowisp

Source: https://nillowisp.github.io/
Author: nill
Description: a quiet digital garden kept by nill.
Language: en

welcome. this is a small garden for thoughts that wanted somewhere quieter to become themselves.

here you'll find specks of different minds, unfamiliar bodies, odd little interfaces, and the unpredictable ways they keep brushing against one another. mainly scattered notes with a sprinkle of essays unhurriedly gathered.

[the garden](https://nillowisp.github.io/garden.md) holds everything by the last time it was pruned.
[the pond](https://nillowisp.github.io/pond.md) keeps its own, more particular weather for the dead.
[the gardener](https://nillowisp.github.io/about.md) isn't really watching.

follow whatever glints.
