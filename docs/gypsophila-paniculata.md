What are these flowers
Why are they here
Who are they for
Her?
What about her
Why buy her flowers
When you're not giving them to her

Look how small they are
Tiny white petals
Of everlasting love
Of absolute devotion
Of things you can't be together

It's better like this
When you're the only one wilting
So that they can bloom for her
In the solitude of your self-made [cage](https://nillowisp.github.io/cage.md)
Where you're the only one questioning
If you love her

They're dying in a cracked vase on your desk
You think you do
But that's your curse
She can never know
Because you do
