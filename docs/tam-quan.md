I was contemplating how to express the way I see the world properly when it struck me that the English word *worldview* doesn't encompass the things I wanted to convey.

For instance, when I think about [continuity](https://nillowisp.github.io/continuity.md), I don't think of it as a general viewpoint of what lies beyond cessation. It comes naturally to me as a synergistic condensation of who I am as a person interacting with the conditions of the world around me as they exist.

*Tam quan* fits how my own brain deconstructs the way I see the world much better.
